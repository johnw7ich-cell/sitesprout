常見痛點 / Common pain points

沒有美國主體，就開不了收款

No US entity → no US Stripe / TikTok Shop in many cases. Without a US account, currency conversion alone can cost 2–5% per transaction.

平台審核各有門檻

Platform gates differ: Shopify needs no EIN to open a store, but most payment tools need US tax documents; Amazon is easier with a US entity; Walmart usually requires a US entity + EIN; TikTok Shop US is the strictest.

網站、收款、平台要一次過對齊

A brand site (T10), a payment route, and a marketplace route must line up — otherwise the site launches but cannot collect or sell properly.

FAQ & Disclaimer

Questions, answered plainly

Iron rule 1 — What is fixed in a build package?

Page count, revision rounds, and support period are fixed in the contract: Launch 5–8 pages, 2 revision rounds, 30 days support; Store up to 30 products, 2 revision rounds, launch support. Beyond scope is quoted separately. There is no bottomless scope.

Iron rule 2 — What do you promise?

We promise work delivery only: the listed build work, the listed monthly SEO/GEO work, and monthly reporting. We never promise third-party approval, Google rankings, AI citations, or revenue results — for SEO/GEO or for overseas consultation.

What is GEO?

GEO = Generative Engine Optimisation: making your business visible in AI search answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI search), through structured content, entity clarity, FAQs, and citations — not by gaming an AI model, and never with a promised citation.

What do I pay separately from Launch / Store?

Domain, hosting, Shopify plan fees, card fees, ad spend, and any Pro / paid theme licence (one licence per Shopify store). These are your costs, in your name, unless a written quote says otherwise.

Who owns my website?

You do, 100%. Domain and hosting are in your name. WordPress sites (T1–T3, T5–T9) and Shopify stores (T4, T10) are delivered to you — SiteSprout does not hold your site hostage for a platform rent.

Phase 1 vs Phase 2 overseas service — what is the difference?

Phase 1 (now): consultation only. No filing, no filing fee, no filing price quoted, no approval promise. Phase 2 (Coming Soon): formal filing for company registration, EIN, bank account, platform onboarding, and long-term accompaniment. Phase 2 is not delivered in Phase 1.

How does the consultation bot work, and when does a human take over?

The bot answers common process questions first. It hands off to a professional human customer service team when: you ask for a human; the question is beyond the FAQ; individual tax / legal / eligibility judgement is needed; you ask about approval outcomes; you ask for a filing price or order; or sensitive documents are involved. The bot never gives individual tax/legal conclusions and never collects passwords or full ID documents.

Is the consultation legal or tax advice?

No. Phase 1 consultation covers general process information only. Individual cases are decided by your accountant, lawyer, bank, or the platform itself.

Can I cancel a monthly retainer?

SEO + GEO retainers have a minimum initial term of 3 months, then roll monthly. Care is monthly and separate from SEO/GEO work.
